This tool needs a small free database to remember your links. It takes about five minutes, once.

  1. Create a Neon project. Sign in at neon.com (free) and make a new project.
  2. Copy the connection string. On the project page, press Connect and copy the long postgresql://… text.
  3. Add two settings in Vercel. Project, Settings, Environment Variables: DATABASE_URL (the string you copied) and SZV_LINK_PIN (a PIN only you know, 8 or more digits).
  4. Redeploy. Vercel, Deployments, the three dots, Redeploy. Then come back here.
