How it works

Every Hebrew letter has a number. Add the letters up and you get the word’s value. Nikud, spaces, punctuation, geresh and gershayim are ignored. Typing in English letters works too: they are turned into Hebrew first (the same rules as Hebrew Type).

Numbers

5787 → ה׳תשפ״ז. 15 and 16 are written ט״ו and ט״ז (not י״ה / י״ו). A geresh after the first letters marks thousands when you type a numeral back.

Matches

A small hand-checked list of well-known words, names, parshiyos and Chabad terms. It is not a dictionary: no match just means nothing on the list has that value.
